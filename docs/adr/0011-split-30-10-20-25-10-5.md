# Revenue split moves to 30/10/20/25/10/5

Status: Supersedes ADR 0003.

The target split is now 30 auditor / 10 contributor / 20 maintainer / 25 adversarial reviewer /
10 treasury / 5 ops (per 1,000 µUSDC: 300 / 100 / 200 / 250 / 100 / 50). The auditor share moves
from 40% to 30% to fund a larger adversarial reviewer pool, which moves from 15% to 25%. A
larger pool gives a stronger incentive for the second, adversarial review that catches a
captured or sloppy first auditor. The MVP split changes with it: auditor 30%, ops 70% (per
1,000: 300 / 700), since the MVP onboards only the auditor and ops roles. The 70% stays ops
income, not a debt owed to anyone, until the other roles launch (invariant 8).

This changed MainNet and TestNet together (docs/TASK.md P8d): the contract is rebuilt with the
30/70 split, and TestNet moves to a new app from that build. The earlier TestNet app
(772553842, 40/60, ADR 0003) is retired.
