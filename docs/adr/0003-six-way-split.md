# Six-way revenue split with a contributor share

Status: Superseded by ADR 0011.

The split is 40 auditor / 10 contributor / 20 maintainer / 15 adversarial reviewer /
10 treasury / 5 ops. The auditor share went down from 50 to fund the author of a merged fix
PR. The completeness of a fix is verified by the maintainer who merges it. In the MVP, only the
auditor and ops roles are onboarded. The other shares are ops income until their roles are
onboarded. This keeps the MVP simple.
